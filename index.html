10Good to Know

Questions & Answers

Quick answers below. Still have a question? Send us a text or a WhatsApp message.

Where is La Redonda?

2695 NE 188th St, Aventura, FL 33180, right next door to Drew Jordan Basketball.

How do I book the field?

Call (786) 969-0588 or email gonzame@laredondaindoorsoccer.com. Rentals start from $130 per hour. Call to confirm availability and current pricing.

Is the field air-conditioned?

Yes. The field is indoors and climate-controlled, so heat, rain and lightning don't stop the game.

Can I host a birthday party?

Yes. Call (786) 969-0588 to plan a soccer birthday party on the field.

What programs are there for kids?

La Redonda FC After-School Soccer for ages 2–6, i9 Sports® youth leagues (Soccer, Flag Football and Multi-Sport), and private training with Coach Sebastien for ages 3 and up.

How do I sign up for i9 Sports?

Register on i9sports.com, or call Coach Mike at 305-239-0731 with questions.

Do you offer private lessons?

Yes. Coach Sebastien offers individual, small-group and futsal sessions. Call or text (786) 489-3702.

Are there restrooms and water fountains?

Yes, both are on site.

Is La Redonda in Aventura or Miami?

Aventura. The field is at 2695 NE 188th St, Aventura, FL 33180.

How much does it cost to rent the field?

Field rentals start from $130 per hour. Call (786) 969-0588 to confirm availability and current pricing.

Can adults rent the field?

Yes. Teams, friends and companies rent the whole field by the hour for 4v4 and 5v5 games.

Is the field open every day?

Yes, the field is open 7 days a week. Call (786) 969-0588 for available times.

Can I message you on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp or text (786) 969-0588, or use the Message Us card on this page.

How do I become a member?

Join for free with the form on this page. Members get $10 off their first registration for La Redonda or i9 Sports programs at La Redonda, plus season updates, free clinics and members-only events like tournament teams and special guest coaches.
